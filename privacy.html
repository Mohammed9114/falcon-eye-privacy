Privacy Policy & Google Play Data Safety Disclosures — Falcon Eye AI

This Privacy Policy explains how Falcon Eye AI (عين الصقر) (Android Package ID: com.cryptopulseai.app) collects, uses, secures, and deletes user data. This policy strictly reflects the actual implementation of our application using Firebase Authentication, Google Cloud Firestore, and Google Play Billing, in full compliance with Google Play Data Safety requirements.

We collect only the minimum data necessary to authenticate your account, verify subscriptions, and synchronize your in-app preferences. We do not share personal data with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device Only

  • Virtual Paper Trading, Price Alerts & AI Chat History: Simulated paper-trading positions, custom price alerts, virtual bot orders, and AI Assistant conversation history are saved locally on your device (localStorage) and are not stored in our cloud databases.
  • AI Market Analysis Prompts: Prompts submitted to the in-app AI Assistant are transmitted securely over HTTPS to our backend solely to generate the immediate response and are never sold or shared with advertisers.

3. Data & Permissions We Never Collect

  • No Advertising ID (AD_ID) or Hardware Identifiers: The app does not declare or use com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID, contains no advertising SDKs, and never collects IMEI, MAC addresses, or ANDROID_ID.
  • No Third-Party Crash Logs or Analytics Telemetry: We do not collect crash logs, diagnostics, or app performance telemetry via third-party trackers (no Crashlytics, Sentry, or Firebase Analytics).
  • No Sensitive Device Permissions: We never request or access GPS location, contacts, camera, microphone, SMS, call logs, calendar, or health data.
  • No Custodial Keys or Payment Card Credentials: Falcon Eye AI is an analytical market intelligence tool. We never ask for exchange private API keys, wallet seed phrases, or payment card numbers (all subscription payments are processed directly by Google Play Billing).

4. Data Security & Encryption in Transit

  • Encryption in Transit: 100% of network traffic between the application, Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and our backend APIs is encrypted in transit using HTTPS / TLS.
  • Access Controls: User records in Google Cloud Firestore are isolated by authenticated User ID (UID) and protected by strict server-side verification and Firestore security rules.

5. Data Retention, User Rights & Account / Data Deletion

  • Retention Policy: User accounts are not automatically deleted when a 30-minute free trial or subscription expires so that users can renew or restore purchases via Google Play at any time.
  • How to Request Account & Data Deletion: Users can request complete deletion of their account and personal data at any time via either method:
    1. In-App: Open Account / Settings → Privacy & Compliance → Request Account & Data Deletion (submits directly to POST /api/account/delete-request).
    2. Without Signing In (Web / Email): Click the button below or send an email to baz.9114@gmail.com with your registered email address, or contact Telegram support at @Moh9114.
  • Deletion SLA: Upon receiving a deletion request, we permanently delete your Firebase Authentication account, profile record, synced preferences, watchlists, and in-app search history within 48 hours. Only anonymized cryptographic billing transaction hashes are retained where required for Google Play financial accounting and fraud prevention compliance.
